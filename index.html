01 — About

A bit about me

I got into code by accident. A college project broke three days before submission, the stylesheet refused to cooperate, and I stayed up until 4am fixing it. I never really stopped.

These days I work across the stack — React and TypeScript on the front, Node and Postgres behind it — but the part I care about most is the seam where design meets engineering. That's where products either feel considered or feel cheap.

Outside the editor: filter coffee, long walks without headphones, and an ongoing argument with my own to-do list. I read changelogs for fun, which I accept is not a personality.
